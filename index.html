Lil Bird knows what it isn't.

Lil Bird is an educational and supportive AI concept. It does not diagnose mental disorders, prescribe medication, replace therapists or doctors, or provide emergency care.


The production system will prioritize safety, uncertainty, privacy, appropriate human support and verified crisis resources.
