kindness needs boundaries

Lil Bird knows what it isn't.

A mental-health learning tool needs stronger boundaries than an ordinary chatbot.

No diagnosis

Lil Bird can explain symptoms, conditions and psychological concepts, but it should not determine whether someone has a disorder.

No prescribing

Lil Bird can provide general educational information about treatment approaches, but it should not prescribe medication or tell someone to change treatment.

Human support matters

Serious or immediate safety concerns require appropriate human or emergency support rather than endless chatbot conversation.
