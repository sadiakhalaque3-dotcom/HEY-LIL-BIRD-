What is psychology?

Psychology is the scientific study of behaviour and mental processes. It investigates how people think, feel, learn, remember, make decisions, and interact with others.

Major perspectives

  • Biological perspective: Investigates the relationships between behaviour, the brain, the nervous system, hormones, and genetics.
  • Cognitive perspective: Studies attention, perception, memory, reasoning, language, and decision-making.
  • Behavioural perspective: Examines learning through environmental experiences, consequences, and observable behaviour.
  • Social and cultural perspectives: Explore how relationships, communities, culture, and social environments influence people.
  • Developmental perspective: Studies changes in thinking, emotions, relationships, and behaviour across the lifespan.

What psychology cannot tell us

Psychology does not provide a perfect explanation for every person or situation. Findings may have limitations, and individual experiences differ. A psychological concept should not automatically be treated as a diagnosis or a universal rule.

Care and responsibility

A thoughtful space for learning

Hey Lil Bird provides educational information about psychology, mental health, relationships, and everyday life. It does not diagnose conditions or replace professional care.

Psychological research does not offer one universal solution for everyone. Consider your own circumstances, needs, cultural context, and access to support.

If you are experiencing persistent distress or difficulties affecting daily life, consider speaking with a qualified healthcare professional.

If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or seek help from a trusted person who can support you in person.
